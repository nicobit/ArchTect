---
title: Status Bar
description: The status bar at the bottom of the ArchTect window.
---

The status bar runs along the bottom of the window and shows workspace-level status at a glance.

![Status bar showing workspace name and Problems indicator](/images/screenshots/portal-guide/status-bar.png)

| Section | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Left** | The current workspace name |
| **Right** | A **Problems** indicator — click it to see DSL validation errors and warnings |

When the canvas has a selected element, the status bar's far right also shows its position and size (`x`, `y`, `w`, `h`) while dragging or resizing.
